Connect the projector

Phone: connect with a USB-C to HDMI adapter (or Lightning AV adapter), or cast or AirPlay the screen. The phone screen becomes the projector frame, so map with this app full screen in landscape. Wired is best: no lag, and sound goes over HDMI.

Laptop with the projector as a second display: open the output window, drag it onto the projector and click it once to go full screen. Keep editing here.
